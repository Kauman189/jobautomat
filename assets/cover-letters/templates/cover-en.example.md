## Usage instructions

The full letter (greeting + body + sign-off, not counting the signature) must
land between **120 and 180 words**. Fill the five slots, then delete this block.

### The slots

- **{conexion}** — 1-2 sentences. Why you're interested + a concrete link to the
  role: what the posting says and why it fits you. Example: "the role joins
  design and implementation, which is how I work day to day". Don't claim
  knowledge of a company you haven't researched.
- **{evidencias}** — 2-3 sentences with action verbs (designed, built, led,
  integrated, documented). Pick two or three real pieces of evidence, not a
  technology list.
- **{cierre}** — 1 sentence. Propose the next step naturally (reviewing the
  portfolio, a call), without exaggerated enthusiasm.

### Which evidence to pick (documented in the CV)

- **B2B payments / SaaS (Acme Corp):** "I designed and built the front end of a
  B2B payments product, taking Figma flows into reusable Angular components."
- **Internal tools (TimeTrack System):** "I designed capture flows, inline
  editing and validation states for an internal time-tracking tool."
- **Mobile-first (Nimbus Coworking):** "I worked on personas, information
  architecture and wireframes for a mobile-first space-booking experience."
- **End-to-end design + build (Northwind):** "I led end-to-end design and
  development, from wireframes and a Figma design system to front end."
- **Landing + dashboard (ClipForge):** "I led the visual design of a landing page
  and an analytics dashboard, including front-end components."

### What NOT to write

- Invented metrics (users, conversion, % improvement, time saved).
- Buzzwords like "front/ui/ux" without explaining the work.
- Technology lists that don't explain the fit.
- Claiming knowledge of a company you haven't researched.
- Signing as founder, Senior Product Designer or team lead without evidence.
- Exaggerated enthusiasm ("passionate", "I'd absolutely love to").
- Private relocation plans unless relevant.
